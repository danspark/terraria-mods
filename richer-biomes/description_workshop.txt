[h1]Richer Biomes[/h1]

Richer Biomes rebuilds world generation around large, connected places worth exploring while preserving Terraria's materials, loot, and progression.

[h2]World features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Tall mountain ranges with caves, chambers, climbing routes, ponds, vines, valleys, clouds, and bridges that enter real passages
[*]Large floating highlands with several terrain grammars and only occasional mountain contact
[*]Biome-specific multi-room landmarks, including buried ice igloos, deliberately unsuitable for NPC housing
[*]Occasional forest lakes crossed by terrain-integrated bridges
[*]Organic, multi-scale transitions between terrain, walls, structures, and neighboring biomes
[*]One guaranteed surface mine feeding an interconnected rail district into the Cavern layer
[/list]

[h2]Before playing[/h2]
Richer Biomes affects world creation. Generate a new world after enabling the mod; existing worlds are not retrofitted.

Secret world seeds are currently left unchanged because their generation and progression rules need dedicated support.

The mod does not add special loot or replace the Dungeon, Jungle Temple, Aether, chests, wiring, or vanilla progression structures.
